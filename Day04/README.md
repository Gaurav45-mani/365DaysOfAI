## 🧠 Key Concepts

### Bernoulli Distribution

A Bernoulli Distribution models an experiment with **only two possible outcomes**:

* **1 → Success**
* **0 → Failure**

The probability of success is **p**, and the probability of failure is **1 − p**.

---

### Probability Mass Function (PMF)

The PMF gives the probability of each possible outcome in a Bernoulli experiment.

* **P(X = 1) = p**
* **P(X = 0) = 1 − p**

---

### Mean

The expected value of a Bernoulli random variable is:

* **Mean = p**

It represents the average outcome over many trials.

---

### Variance

Variance measures the spread of outcomes.

* **Variance = p(1 − p)**

## 🎯 Key Takeaways

* Bernoulli Distribution is the simplest **discrete probability distribution**.
* It is used when an experiment has **exactly two outcomes**.
* It serves as the foundation for the **Binomial Distribution** and many machine learning classification problems.
