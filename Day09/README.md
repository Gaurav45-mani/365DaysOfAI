## 🧠 Central Limit Theorem (CLT)

The **Central Limit Theorem** states that when we repeatedly take sufficiently large random samples from a population and calculate their **sample means**, the distribution of those sample means becomes approximately **normally distributed**, even if the original population is not normally distributed.

> **Important:** CLT is about the distribution of **sample means**, not the original population.
